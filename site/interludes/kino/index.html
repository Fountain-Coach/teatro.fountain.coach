An acoustic interlude

Kino After Dark

Geräuschumgebung · a cinema after its film

∞NO MEASURE / ONLY TIME
After the filmListen when you wish

After the last scene

The film is over. The room retains something of it: an almost-kiss, a hand withdrawn, the silence before tears.

No rhythm insists on being heard. The air moves, the old projector leaves a little dust, and fragments appear only at a distance.

No passing soundsEar observes the room · MIDI 2.0 carries the echo

Original Csound cinema air and film dust, recomposed as slow non-periodic grains. Optional quiet traces from the attributed Satzfunk archive are off by default. Ear measures the dry sound and returns MIDI 2.0 CC74 to a bounded echo. No bass sequence, drums, pads, chords or borrowed film voices. The public edition has not changed.
